Luanti Map & Biome Explorer

The modern, accessible, open-source Seed Biome Generator, Interactive Voronoi Inspector, and SQLite World Map Viewer for Luanti (formerly Minetest) players, server administrators, and mod developers.

Procedural Seed & Biome Synthesis

Preview infinite Luanti worlds directly from any seed (similar to Amidst for Minetest). Evaluates authentic 2D/3D Octave Perlin Noise fields for terrain elevation, heat, and humidity with support for Mapgen v7, Valleys, Carpathian, and Flat parameters.

Interactive Heat/Humidity Voronoi Matrix

Inspect the exact mathematical Voronoi tessellation that governs Luanti biome distribution. Features a live synchronized crosshair that tracks your map cursor across Heat (0–100) and Humidity (0–100) cells, with one-click biome isolation and altitude band filtering.

Local World Folder & SQLite DB Reader

Point directly to any local Luanti world folder to automatically parse world.mt, map_meta.txt, and map.sqlite. Decodes 64-bit packed block coordinates and decompresses zstd/zlib MapBlock blobs to render player builds and explored terrain.

3D Topographic Relief & Web Worker Pool

Powered by MapLibre GL JS and a multi-core Web Worker pool. Toggle 3D Terrain-RGB elevation, dynamic hillshading, Voronoi cell contour borders, and MapBlock (16×16) / MapChunk (80×80) coordinate grids at a fluid 60 FPS.
